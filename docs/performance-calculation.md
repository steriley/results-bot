How Performance is calculated
Performance measures how efficiently you convert predictions into points, relative to the maximum you could have scored.
Each prediction is worth a maximum of 10 points for an exact correct score. A correct result (win, lose, or draw) without the exact score earns 3 points, and getting one team's score right earns 1 point.
Your performance score is calculated as your total points divided by the maximum possible points you could have earned across all your predictions, expressed as a percentage. A performance of 100% would mean every prediction was an exact correct score.
This means performance rewards quality over quantity — a player who predicts fewer games but gets them right will outscore someone who predicts everything but guesses wildly.
